What the Ghost Reactor actually is

A co-op mode where you and your squad are employees, not players.

Another Axiom launched it on 2 May 2025 and rebuilt it on 17 October 2025. It sits deep in the Mines and replaced the old Deep Mines area. You arrive in a hub full of machines, posters and doors, then take an elevator down into the job.

It is a roguelike, so the mine rearranges itself every shift. You never learn a route, only how to read a room quickly. You can bring a squad or take a shift solo if you want it harder.

Pay and paperwork

Three separate things get counted, and people mix them up constantly.

Cores are what ghosts drop. They power the drill and can be turned into credits. Credits buy your gear from the TOOL-O-MATIC during a shift. Shiny rocks are the normal Gorilla Tag currency, and they buy the permanent upgrades like personal drop pods that put you straight onto lower levels.

Cores also come out of crates and off the plants growing in the tunnels, so look at the walls, not just at the ghosts.

How one shift runs

Same four beats every time, however deep you get.

  1. Gear up at the hub

    The drill floor has the TOOL-O-MATIC 2.0, the Recycler, the Career Hub and the Juice-O-Matic. Spend before you drop, because the elevator does not wait for you.

  2. Clear the zone

    Ghosts patrol until they notice you. Banish them with the baton, blind them with the flash, then pick the cores up off the floor before something else does.

  3. Hit the quota

    The drill needs a set number of cores before it moves. Everyone's haul counts toward the same total, so a squad that splits up and sweeps beats a squad that sticks together.

  4. Ride it down

    Quota met, the drill digs and the next zone opens a thousand metres lower. Deeper zones pay better and hit harder. Repeat until the shift ends or the shift ends you.

Standard issue equipment

Company policy is that all of this is perfectly safe.

Charged Baton

Your main tool. Hit a ghost with it and the ghost becomes a spectral core you can carry. Needs recharging, which is what collectors are for.

Melee

Spectral Flash

A burst of light that deals with ghosts you would rather not get close to. Good for clearing a doorway you are about to run through.

Ranged

Collector

Hoovers up cores, stores them as energy, and recharges your other gear. Also powers lamps and doors, and cores can be passed between collectors.

Support

Shield Gun

The recommended pick for boss fights. Keep it up during the Monkeye and you can survive attacks that would otherwise end the run.

Defence

Personal Drop Pod

Bought with shiny rocks and parked beside the main pod. Drops you straight to lower levels, and upgrades with juice to go deeper still.

Upgrade

Juice

The upgrade resource. Cheap next to a full kit, but it is what turns a drop pod from a shortcut into a serious head start.

Resource

Known personnel hazards

Five base types, plus the two things management does not call ghosts.

Crawler

Dark purple, skeletal, glowing white eyes, nothing below the waist. Patrols until it sees you, then holds still, winds up, and pounces. Drops a core every time.

Common

Pest

Bug-shaped and fast. There are armoured ones, huge ones worth up to three cores, and an enraged version in the deeper zones that attacks alarmingly quickly.

Common

Lobber

Skeletal like a crawler but it throws instead of pouncing. Fight it from behind cover rather than out in the open.

Ranged

Summoner

The one to kill first. Leave it alone and the room refills faster than your squad can clear it.

Priority

Phantom

A miner with a skeletal head, a pickaxe for one arm and a drill for the other. Only one per zone, drops nothing, and cannot be killed. It screams and chases. Leave.

Do not engage

Monkeye

First boss, at 4,000 m. Escaped from the Basement. Fires lasers, slams the ground, and spins with every laser on. Gains 20% more health for each extra monke in the lobby.

Things you can actually try to find

None of this is guaranteed in a given shift. That is the point of looking.

  • The Fake Forest

    10,000 / 15,000 / 20,000 / 25,000 m

    A zone that has a chance to appear at those depths, once per run at most. It is dressed as the Forest, but faceless monkes stand around watching you and the leaderboard prints messages instead of scores. A phantom is usually tucked near the corner, left of the reactor entrance.

  • The Bayou leaderboard

    Bayou zone

    It used to list everyone in the lobby. Now it tells you that you are not supposed to be here, the same line the Fake Forest gives you.

  • Riding the Monkeye

    4,000 m

    If you die in the fight you are not out of the fight. Climb onto the boss and go along for the ride while your squad finishes it.

  • The Meteor Monster

    5,000 / 11,000 / 30,000 m

    It entered orbit in October 2025 and crash-landed in a live event in January 2026. It has eye tentacles like the Monkeye but it is a separate thing. Its wake-up cutscene music opens like the track Curse Of The Monkeye.

  • The bottom of the map

    31,000 m

    The drill stops here. Meet quota at the floor and it lifts back to 30,000 m and digs down again, so there is no thirty-two. Getting here at all is the flex.

  • Halloween staffing

    Seasonal

    Lucy turns up in the Reactor during Halloween and does not follow ghost rules. She can lift you while a ghost is next to you, which is as bad as it sounds.
